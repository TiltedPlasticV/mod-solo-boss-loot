Items table

Extra item per kill, by creature level

How often a kill adds an item (median over the loot tables in each level band). Any rare item is before the wants check; the player lines only count items that player wants, since unwanted items just don't drop. MaxCombinedChance lifts every line. Tables that are already above it get nothing, which pulls the median down.

Table

How loot tables end up

Hits target: scaled so a rare item drops MaxCombinedChance of the time. Every item capped: too few rare items to reach the target even with each at MaxItemChance, so they all sit at MaxItemChance. Already above target: the normal chances already reach MaxCombinedChance, so the creature gets nothing extra.

What the extra items are

Share of extra items by quality (each loot table counts equally), and how many are recipes. QualityWeights and RecipeWeight move these directly. With a low CompressionRatio the original rarity still matters a lot, so the many common greens take most of the budget.

Chance of one specific item

Chance that a kill drops one particular rare item, over every creature and item pair: the line is the middle half (25th to 75th percentile), the dot the median. Gray is the normal chance, color is after the boost. Hover a row for "1 in N kills".

Every rare item: normal chance vs boosted chance

Each cell counts creature and item pairs, darker is more. On the diagonal nothing changed. CompressionRatio tilts the band flatter (the rarest items gain the most), the weights split it by quality, MaxCombinedChance moves it up and down, and MaxItemChance is the ceiling.
